The Story Behind The Success

From refugee camps to international acclaim – a journey of resilience, determination, and unwavering commitment to excellence.

Biography

A Life of Purpose

Born in 1964 in India, Shiv Khera's early life was marked by challenges that would define his character and mission. His family migrated to India during the partition, and he grew up understanding the value of hard work, perseverance, and integrity from a young age.

After completing his education, Shiv began his career in the corporate world, quickly rising through the ranks. However, he felt a calling to make a larger impact. In 1993, he made the pivotal decision to dedicate his life to empowering others through his writings and speeches.

Today, Shiv Khera has addressed over 5 million people across 60+ countries, worked with Fortune 500 companies, and his books have sold more than 20 million copies in 20+ languages. He remains committed to his mission of creating a better world through individual transformation.
