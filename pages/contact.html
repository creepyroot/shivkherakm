FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book Shiv for a speaking engagement?

+

We recommend booking at least 3-6 months in advance for speaking engagements, especially for peak seasons (September-November and January-March). However, we do have some flexibility for last-minute opportunities. Please contact us to check availability.

Do you offer virtual training programs?

+

Yes! We offer comprehensive virtual training programs that are just as impactful as in-person sessions. Our online platforms include interactive elements, breakout sessions, and follow-up support to ensure maximum engagement and learning retention.

Can training programs be customized for our organization?

+

Absolutely. All corporate training programs can be tailored to address your organization's specific challenges, culture, and goals. We begin with a needs assessment to understand your requirements and then design a program that delivers maximum ROI.

Are books available for international shipping?

+

Yes, we ship books worldwide. Shipping times and costs vary by location. Bulk orders (50+ copies) qualify for discounted rates. Contact our sales team for custom quotes on large orders.

Do you offer certification programs?

+

Yes, we offer certification programs for individuals who want to become certified trainers in Shiv Khera's methodologies. These intensive programs include comprehensive training, assessment, and ongoing support. Contact us for details on upcoming certification cohorts.
