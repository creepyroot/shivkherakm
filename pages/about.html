About Shiv Khera

A journey of perseverance, excellence, and unwavering commitment to empowering others

From Humble Beginnings to Global Impact

Born in 1965 in India, Shiv Khera's early life was marked by challenges that would later become the foundation of his philosophy. Orphaned at a young age, he learned the value of self-reliance and determination.

After completing his education, Shiv Khera started his career in the insurance industry, where he quickly rose to become one of the top performers. However, his true calling was to inspire and empower others to achieve their full potential.

In 1993, he founded his training and consulting company, and since then, he has touched millions of lives through his books, seminars, and corporate training programs across 40+ countries.
