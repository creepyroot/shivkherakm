Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I book Shiv Khera for a speaking engagement?

Fill out the contact form above and select "Speaking Engagement" as your inquiry type. Our team will respond within 48 hours with availability and details.

What are the fees for corporate training programs?

Corporate training fees vary based on program duration, customization level, and location. Contact us for a customized quote.

Are there group discounts for programs?

Yes, we offer special rates for groups of 5 or more. Contact our team for group pricing details.

Can programs be customized for our organization?

Absolutely! We specialize in creating tailored programs that address your organization's specific challenges and goals.

What is the response time for inquiries?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24-48 business hours. Urgent requests are prioritized.

Do you offer virtual training options?

Yes, all our programs are available in both in-person and virtual formats to accommodate global audiences.
