Who is Shiv Khera?

Shiv Khera is an international bestselling author, motivational speaker, and business consultant. With over three decades of experience, he has empowered millions of people worldwide to achieve success in their personal and professional lives.

His books have been translated into multiple languages and have sold millions of copies globally. He has addressed Fortune 500 companies, government organizations, and educational institutions across 40+ countries.

Shiv Khera's mission is simple: to help people realize their true potential and live fulfilling lives through practical wisdom and actionable strategies.
